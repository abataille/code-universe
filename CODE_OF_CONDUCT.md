# Code of Conduct

Code Universe adopts the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

## Our commitment

We pledge to make project participation welcoming, safe, and equitable. Be
respectful, assume good intent without dismissing impact, give constructive
feedback, protect private information, and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks,
trolling, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone officially represents Code Universe or VCLab.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban a participant
when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the
[VCLab contact page](https://www.vclab.com/contact.html). Reports will be handled
as confidentially as reasonably possible. Do not open a public issue containing
sensitive personal information.
